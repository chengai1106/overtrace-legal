Restoring a purchase on a new device

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.

The camera view is black

Overtrace needs camera permission to show the live view. Open the iOS Settings app → Overtrace → enable Camera.

My reference keeps moving

Tap Lock at the top right. While locked, the reference will not move, scale, or rotate until you unlock it.

Where are my images stored

Only on your device. Nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them.
