Restoring Pro on a new device

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that holds the subscription or Lifetime purchase.

How do I start drawing on paper?

  1. Choose a photo. In version 1.3.2, tap Prepare to draw, then Start camera. In version 1.3.1, tap Start Camera.
  2. Secure your iPhone above the paper, with its rear camera facing the page. Allow Camera access when prompted.
  3. Line up the reference on screen and adjust its opacity. In version 1.3.2, tap Lock reference at the bottom of the tracing view; in version 1.3.1, tap Lock at the top right.
  4. Watch the screen while drawing on paper. If the phone or paper moves, unlock and realign the reference before continuing.

Version 1.3.2 also includes Phone setup and Tracing basics in the app.

The camera view is black

Overtrace needs camera permission to show the live view. Open the iOS Settings app → Overtrace → enable Camera.

My reference keeps moving

After aligning the photo, use Lock reference at the bottom of the tracing view in version 1.3.2, or Lock at the top right in version 1.3.1. Lock prevents on-screen move, scale and rotate gestures. It does not keep the reference aligned if the phone or paper physically moves; realign it before continuing.

Where are my images stored

Only on your device. Nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them.
