Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

Overtrace does not require an account and never uploads, sells, or shares your personal photos or drawing references. There is no advertising and no cross-app tracking.

What stays on your device

Reference images you add and the work settings Overtrace saves for them are stored locally by the app on your device (standard iOS device backups may include local app storage unless disabled in iOS Settings). Overtrace does not upload that library anywhere. Deleting the app removes this local data.

Camera and photos

Camera access is used only to show the live view you trace on top of or to capture a reference photo when you choose. Live camera frames are never recorded or transmitted by Overtrace. Photo library access is used only when you choose a picture to use as a reference. If the selected picture is stored in iCloud Photos, Apple Photos may retrieve it before passing the selected image to Overtrace.

Diagnostics and tracking

Overtrace does not track you across apps or websites. Pseudonymous product-interaction and subscription-plan outcome diagnostics (such as screen flow, plan selected, verified purchase completion, or restore outcome) are sent to PostHog EU together with a randomly generated app-installation UUID stored in local UserDefaults. That installation identifier allows Overtrace to measure multi-session funnel reliability within a single app installation, is deleted when the app is uninstalled, and is never linked to a user account, name, email address, Apple Account, or advertising identifier. Accordingly, the app's privacy manifest declares NSPrivacyTracking = false, unlinked non-tracking NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypeDeviceID, NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypeProductInteraction, and NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypePurchaseHistory (NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypeLinked = false) for analytics, and a single accessed API category — UserDefaults, declared under reason CA92.1, used to remember your own settings and random installation UUID on this device.

Purchases

Weekly, monthly, and annual subscriptions and the one-time Lifetime purchase are billed and processed by Apple. Overtrace never sees your payment card or Apple Account identity; only pseudonymous plan-tier and purchase/restore outcome events keyed to the random local install identifier are recorded for app analytics as described above. Apple's privacy terms govern App Store billing transactions.

Children

Overtrace is rated 4+ and contains no user-generated social content, no social features, and no advertising.
